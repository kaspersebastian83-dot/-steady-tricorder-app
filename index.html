Evidence & your records

Recorded values, personal comparisons and published guidance are separate. Comparisons use available observations, not a diagnosis or a live medical scan.
  • Sleep: adult consensus recommends at least 7 hours of sleep per night on a regular basis. PMID 26039963.
  • Steps: cohort meta-analyses show a dose-response relationship; health benefit is seen below 10,000 steps/day and there is no single clinical pass/fail cutoff. PMID 37555441; PMID 36537288.
  • Wearables: steps and heart rate can be useful trend measures, while energy-expenditure estimates are much less reliable. Wearable stress/HRV models have generalization limits. PMID 32897239; PMID 36893657.
  • Weight: regular self-weighing can support weight management, but scale change is not a direct measurement of fat change. PMID 25521523.
  • Waist-to-height: a systematic review supports 0.5 as a simple cardiometabolic screening boundary; this is risk context, not diagnosis. PMID 20819243.
  • STOP-Bang: high sensitivity but low specificity; useful for screening, not diagnosis. PMID 34406554; PMID 33664122.
  • Home BP: structured multi-day home monitoring is preferred over isolated readings; a systematic review found 3 days captures much of the available prognostic information, with 7 days useful near thresholds. PMID 30668627. Current European hypertension guidance supports home/ambulatory monitoring. A review summarizing ESC/ESH guidance uses <135/85 mmHg as normal out-of-office BP. PMID 39210715; PMID 37161879.
Evidence audit date: 2026-09-22. Thresholds are intentionally conservative and may be updated as guidance changes.

The reference library is not updated automatically. The app is not clinically validated. Data-coverage minimums (3 observations for weekly comparisons; 10 historical observations for personal baselines) are display rules, not clinical cutoffs.

Sound starts off. Turn it on to hear interface chirps, a scan sweep, a quiet scanning hum and the completion chime. All sounds are synthesized locally. Nothing uses your microphone.

Opening and installing
This file must run in a real browser, not a picture or attachment preview. The ZIP contains an installable web-app package and a desktop launcher. A phone home-screen installation needs a stable HTTPS website; this download does not deploy one.
